HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move WASD or arrows
Attack Space
Ability Q (riff stun or shield bash)
Special R (heal song or guard)
Pause P (either hero can pause for both)
Dash Shift (brief invulnerability)
On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

GOAL

Clear each room, get the key from the Keeper in the Crypt, then reach the exit together. Smash chests for gold (beware of mimics), spend it at rest stops, and beat the dragon.

TIPS

Pick a difficulty when you create a room: Easy has more hearts and softer monsters, Hard has fewer hearts and tougher ones (with a score bonus). Watch for the Iron Warden mini-boss in the Treasure Chamber. When a run ends, press PLAY AGAIN to go straight back in. Red rings mean a hit is coming. Stand next to a fallen friend to revive them. Hearts can lift you above your starting 5.
